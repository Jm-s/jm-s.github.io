### Key findings

- MAGNIMS addresses MRI use across diagnosis, attacks, recovery and research.
- Standardized core and optional sequences are proposed.
- Seronegative NMOSD remains a classification challenge.

### Interpretation and limitations

**Editorial interpretation:** separating the indication for MRI from the acquisition protocol should make local implementation more explicit. Before adopting a protocol, check the complete sequence table, timing recommendations and evidence supporting each recommendation.

The accessible abstract does not establish exact scan intervals, sequence parameters, diagnostic accuracy or evidence grades. These should not be inferred from this summary. Expert recommendations also should not be treated as evidence that an imaging strategy improves patient outcomes.

### Take-home message

The framework organizes MRI around clinical purpose and disease phase. Consult the full recommendations before changing acquisition or follow-up protocols.
